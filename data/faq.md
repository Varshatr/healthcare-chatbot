## 4. Visiting Hours & Inpatient Guidelines

### Visiting Hours by Ward
- **General Inpatient Wards:** 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM - 7:30 PM daily. Maximum 2 visitors permitted at the patient bedside at one time.
- **Intensive Care Units (ICU / CCU / CTVS):** 12:00 PM - 1:00 PM and 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM daily. Only 1 immediate family member permitted. Masks and sterile covers are provided and mandatory.
- **Neonatal & Pediatric ICU (NICU / PICU):** 11:30 AM - 12:30 PM and 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM. Parents are permitted 24/7 with a designated pass.
- **Maternity & Private Deluxe Suites:** 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM - 8:00 PM. One registered primary attendant is allowed 24 hours.

### Visitor Rules
1. All visitors must obtain a digital or paper visitor pass from the Ground Floor Visitor Registration Desk.
2. Children under the age of 12 are not permitted in Critical Care Units (ICU/CCU).
3. Hand hygiene: Sanitize hands at bedside dispensers before and after visiting patients.
4. Quiet hours are strictly observed hospital-wide between 8:00 PM and 7:00 AM.
5. Outside cooked food and fresh flowers are strictly prohibited in inpatient rooms.
6. Apex Care Hospital is a 100% tobacco-free and smoke-free campus.
7. Photography or recording of patients, staff, or medical equipment is strictly prohibited.

## 7. Appointment Booking

- **How to Book:** Appointments can be booked on our website under the "Book Appointment" tab, by phone through the central OPD desk at `+1 (800) 467-7000`, or in-person at the Ground Floor OPD Registration Counter.
- **What to Bring:** Please arrive 15 minutes before your scheduled slot. Bring a valid Government Photo ID, insurance card (if applicable), and any previous diagnostic records, x-rays, or prescriptions.

## 8. Billing, Insurance & Cashless Hospitalization

- **Cashless Insurance Desk:** Located in Tower A, Ground Floor. Open Monday to Saturday: 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM.
- **Empanelled Insurers:** BlueCross BlueShield, UnitedHealthcare, Aetna Health, Cigna Global, Medicare & Medicaid Services, Star Health & Allied Insurance, HDFC ERGO Health, and Medi Assist TPA.
- **Cashless Admission Process:** Present your health insurance card and photo ID at least 48 hours prior to planned admission (or within 24 hours for emergency admissions). The TPA desk coordinates clinical pre-authorization directly with your insurer.
- **Accepted Payment Modes:** Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Rupay), Instant UPI/QR payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Apple Pay, Paytm), Net Banking, Demand Drafts, Cash, and Direct Cashless Settlement with approved insurers.
- **Cost Estimates:** Formal procedure cost estimates can be obtained from the Billing & Financial Counselling Desk before admission.
